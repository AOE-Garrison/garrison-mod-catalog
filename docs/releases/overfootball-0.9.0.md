# OverFootball 0.9.0

Play stadium football with one to four participants per team. Each team has seven footballers; select a free teammate to control, then switch to another when needed.

How to launch
1. Enable OverFootball in Garrison and press Play.
2. In a custom lobby, select OverFootball map and OverFootball tuning. Keep the regular game mode.
3. Choose Team 1 or Team 2 in the AoE IV lobby and close unused slots.
4. Every human presses READY on the match setup screen.

Controls: right click to move; Q for a weak kick, W for a strong kick, E to boost, R to follow the ball, T to cheer.

The top Stamina Info button toggles both team panels. Stamina bars show the controlling participant's lobby colour. Team kits stay blue/red in team play; 1v1 keeps the chosen lobby colours.

For solo practice, use one human and three to seven AI slots. Practice participants yield control when you select their footballer.
